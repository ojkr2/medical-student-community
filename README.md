# Medical Student Community

A community and educational platform designed for medical students.

## Project Goal

The goal of this project is to provide medical students with a structured environment for learning, practicing medical questions, reviewing educational resources, and tracking their academic progress.

## Features

- Medical student profiles
- Question practice and performance tracking
- Student ranking system
- Educational resources
- Exam-focused study materials
- Community interaction between medical students

## Target Users

Medical students and healthcare learners who want a structured platform for studying, practicing questions, and sharing educational resources.
